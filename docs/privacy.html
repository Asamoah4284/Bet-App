1. Who we are

Quibet (“the App”) is a personal recovery companion that helps people track gambling-free progress, manage urges, keep money notes, connect with accountability buddies, and optionally block gambling websites on the device. The App is developed by Asamoah (“we”, “us”).

Quibet is not a medical device, therapy provider, or crisis service. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately.

2. Information we process

2.1 Stored only on your device

These stay in on-device storage (for example SQLite / AsyncStorage / secure storage) and are not uploaded as part of ordinary use:

  • Urge logs (intensity, optional emotion/location tags, notes)
  • Journal entries and mood notes
  • Money kept / slip logs and savings goal
  • Daily gambling-free reflections used for your streak
  • Safety plan content (reasons and coping actions)
  • Profile photo you choose (device-only)
  • Custom Shield domains you add, and Shield on/off preference
  • Theme, onboarding, and reminder preferences cache

2.2 Stored on our servers (when you create an account)

  • Account identifiers: email, password hash (if you use email/password), Google account ID (if you use Google sign-in)
  • Profile: display name, optional username, optional bio, buddy code
  • Buddy relationships and requests
  • Buddy check-ins you choose to post
  • Optional discovery flag (“allow people to find me”)
  • Optional leaderboard opt-in and a limited streak snapshot for rankings
  • Push notification tokens and notification preferences (if you enable remote reminders)
  • Shared Shield catalog of known betting domains / package names (not your browsing history)

2.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run third-party advertising trackers in the App as described here.
  • Shield filters DNS on-device; we do not upload your browsing history or visited URLs.
  • Leaderboards never include money totals, urge notes, email, or your profile photo.

3. How we use information

  • Provide authentication, buddies, check-ins, and sync of limited recovery stats you choose to share
  • Send optional reminders, encouragement, buddy and milestone notifications
  • Operate Shield’s shared domain list and your on-device blocking when enabled
  • Maintain security, abuse prevention, and basic service reliability
  • Respond to support requests you send us

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

If GDPR/UK GDPR applies, we process personal data based on:

  • Contract — to provide the App features you request
  • Consent — for optional items such as notifications, discovery, leaderboards, Google sign-in, photo library access, and Shield VPN/Network Extension permission
  • Legitimate interests — securing the service and preventing abuse, balanced against your rights

5. Sharing

  • Buddies — people you connect with can see profile fields and check-ins you choose to share
  • Leaderboard (optional) — display name, username, streak only
  • Service providers — hosting (for example cloud database / API hosting), push delivery (Expo / Apple APNs / Google FCM), and Google Sign-In as configured
  • Legal — if required by law or to protect rights and safety

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Permissions the App may request

  • Notifications — reminders and in-app alerts
  • Photos / media library — optional profile photo (stays on device)
  • VPN / local network / Network Extension (Shield) — optional on-device DNS filtering of gambling websites
  • Internet — account, buddies, catalog sync, and push registration

Urge “location” tags in the App are labels you type or choose (for example “home”), not continuous GPS tracking.

7. Data retention

  • On-device data remains until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App
  • Server account data is kept while your account remains active
  • You may email us to request account deletion; we will delete or anonymize server-held personal data associated with your account within a reasonable period, unless retention is required by law

8. Security

We use industry-standard practices such as encrypted transport (HTTPS) in production, hashed passwords, and token-based sessions. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Children’s privacy

Quibet is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. International transfers

Our servers or subprocessors may be located outside your country. Where required, we take appropriate steps to protect personal data during transfer.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data, or to object/restrict certain processing. Contact us using the email above. You may also withdraw optional consents in the App (notifications, discovery, leaderboard, Shield).
